# AI Development Policy Framework

Customizable AI development policy templates for different use cases, aligned with NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, and OECD principles.

## Use Cases Included
- **Hiring AI Tool** (HR/Recruitment): Focuses on fairness, EEOC compliance, disparate impact testing
- **Medical Diagnostic AI** (Healthcare): Prioritizes patient safety, clinical validation, FDA compliance

## Key Features
- Domain-specific risk mitigation
- Lifecycle governance (data → deployment → monitoring)
- Ready-to-adapt approval workflows
- Comparison of HR vs Healthcare priorities

## Compliance Alignment Mapping
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Template Section    | NIST AI RMF                          | EU AI Act (High-Risk)                  | OECD Principles                |
|-------------------- |--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **Ethical Review**  | Govern (risk ID)                     | Art. 9 (risk assessment)               | Human rights, fairness         |
| **Data Practices**  | Map/Measure (data quality, bias)     | High-quality datasets, bias mitigation | Robustness, data governance    |
| **Model Building**  | Measure(fairness),Manage(mitigation) | Accuracy, robustness, human oversight  | Transparency,explainability    |
| **Testing/Validat** | Measure (metrics),Manage(monitoring) | Continuous monitoring, human oversight | Safety, robustness             |
| **Documentation**   | Govern(traceability),Map(modelcards) | Detailed documentation, logging        | Transparency, accountability   |
| **Approval Gates**  | Govern (roles/responsibilities)      | Human oversight, deployer obligations  | Accountability                 |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
